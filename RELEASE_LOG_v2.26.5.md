## 🌟 Overview & Key Updates

Version `v2.26.5` delivers an ultra-premium visual overhaul and micro-animation suite for the **Master GM (Founder & Super Admin)** user profile badge and avatar system.

- **Crown Floating Motion (`crownFloat`):** Added smooth 2.5s floating physics and slight rotational sway to the golden crown icon `👑`.
- **Pulsating Golden Halo (`masterGoldenPulse`):** Applied a breathing gold pulse aura and inner glow ring around the founder's avatar ring.
